# Gulf Crescent Bank — Anti-Money-Laundering Policy (Extract)

Internal policy GCB-AML-2026 rev. 4. Fictional document for demo purposes.

## §3.1 Cash reporting threshold

Single cash transactions of AED 37,500 or more require a Cash Transaction
Report (CTR) filed with the compliance office within 24 hours. Tellers must
record the depositor's Emirates ID for any cash deposit above AED 20,000.

## §4.2 Structuring (smurfing)

Multiple cash deposits individually below the CTR threshold that aggregate to
a significant amount within a short window — particularly when spread across
branches, ATMs, or depositors — constitute suspected structuring. Indicators:

- three or more sub-threshold deposits within five business days;
- rotation across branches or channels without commercial rationale;
- rapid onward transfer of accumulated funds within 72 hours.

Suspected structuring must be escalated to a Level-2 investigation and may not
be dismissed at teller or branch level.

## §5.4 Activity vs. declared profile

Account activity exceeding 200% of declared turnover for two consecutive
months requires a Request for Information (RFI) to the customer and a KYC
refresh. Investigators must compare aggregate monthly credits against the
declared figure recorded at onboarding.

## §6.1 Watchlist and sanctions exposure

Any counterparty — or beneficial owner of a counterparty — matching the
internal watchlist or an international sanctions list triggers mandatory
Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD). Funds already transferred to such counterparties
must be reported; no further outbound transfers may be processed while the
case is open. Secondary-sanctions exposure is treated with the same severity
as a direct match.

## §7.3 Suspicious Activity Report (SAR)

A SAR must be filed with the FIU via goAML within 5 business days of an
investigation concluding that suspicion is substantiated. The SAR narrative
must state: the pattern observed, the amounts and period, the counterparties,
the policy sections engaged, and the recommended account action. Tipping off
the customer is a criminal offence.

## §8.2 Investigation record

Every investigative step — including database queries, document reviews, and
external screenings — must be logged with its evidence in the case file.
